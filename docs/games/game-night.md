# Game Night

**Status:** Playable (v0.1)
**Art:** MAS FunPack (`shared/assets/mas-mini-retro/MASFunPack-v.1.2/AllGames/`)

## Core loop
Pick a table, play a quick round (1–5 minutes), and see your record or best score go up.

## Tables
| Table | Modes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tic-Tac-Toe | vs CPU, 2 players | The CPU plays perfectly 80% of the time, so it's beatable. Players take turns going first. |
| Reversi | vs CPU, 2 players | The CPU looks 3 moves ahead and values corners. Legal moves are shown as dots. |
| Yacht | Solo | 13 rounds, Yahtzee-style scoring, a +35 bonus for 63+ in the upper section, and a saved best score. |

Your win/draw/loss record against the CPU and your best Yacht score are saved on the device.

## Scope limits (v1)
No online play, no accounts, no sound.
